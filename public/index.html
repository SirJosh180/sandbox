How it works

From application to demo day

Every cohort runs like a small software company. You join a cross-functional team, get a real brief, and deliver it in short sprints with mentors reviewing your work.

  1. step 01

    Apply

    Tell us your course, the roles you're curious about, and what you want to learn. No portfolio needed.

    ≈ 10 minutes
  2. step 02

    Get matched

    We build balanced teams of 5–7 so every project has a PM, designers, developers, QA and DevOps.

    one team · one brief
  3. step 03

    Build in sprints

    Plan, stand up, code, review, retro. You use the same tools and rituals that real IT teams use.

    git · tickets · code review
  4. step 04

    Ship and reflect

    Demo your product, get feedback on your role, and decide whether to go deeper or try a new track next cohort.

    demo day + role review

FAQ

Questions students ask first

Do I need experience to join?

No. Beginners are welcome on every track. We ask about your current skills only to balance teams, so each team has a mix of people who are learning and people who can help.

Who can apply?

Any student taking an IT-related course, such as Computer Science, Information Technology, Information Systems or Multimedia, from any year level.

How much time does it take?

Plan for around 6–10 hours a week: one team meeting, a short weekly check-in, and your own task work. You'll tell us your availability in the form.

What kind of projects will I work on?

Practical apps and systems with a real brief, such as booking tools, inventory systems, school org websites and internal dashboards. Each team ships one product per cohort.

Can I switch roles?

Yes. You can shadow another role during a cohort and pick a new primary role in the next one. Finding the role that fits you is the goal.

Is there a fee?

Applying is free. Your organizer will share any program details with you after you're accepted.
